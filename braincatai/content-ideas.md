# BrainCatAI — Content Ideas

Animated AI cat Shorts • Education style • Vertical 9:16 • 30–60 sec

## Core Formula

`Hook (0–2s)` → `Curiosity gap (2–5s)` → `Explanation with cat visuals (5–45s)` → `Mind-blown payoff + CTA (45–60s)`

The cat should *react* (surprised, smug, confused, "aha!") to keep retention. Use the cat as the audience surrogate.

## 8. Series Ideas (recurring formats)

- **"Cat Asks Why"** — 1 question, 1 answer, every Monday
- **"Smart Cat / Dumb Cat"** — myth vs fact, two cat characters
- **"Cat Explains in 30 Sec"** — speed-run a complex topic
- **"What If…?"** — hypothetical scenarios with cat consequences
- **"Today I Learned"** — daily one-fact uploads

## Title Formulas (high-CTR for Shorts)

- `Why [common thing] is actually [surprising truth]`
- `What if [extreme scenario]?`
- `[Number] facts about [topic] that broke my brain`
- `You've been [doing X] wrong your whole life`
- `The reason [thing] exists will shock you`
- `[Famous thing] is a lie. Here's the truth.`

## Hook Lines (first 2 seconds)

- "Cats can't taste sweet. Here's why that matters…"
- "This cat just solved a 200-year-old math problem."
- "99% of people get this wrong."
- "I'm about to ruin your childhood in 30 seconds."
- "Wait — gravity isn't real?"

## Thumbnail Patterns

- Cat with 😱 expression + 1 huge word
- Split screen: cat + the topic (e.g., black hole)
- Cat pointing at giant number/fact
- Use bright color (yellow/red) on edges, not center

---

## Posting Cadence Suggestion

- **Daily** for first 30 days (algorithm boost window)
- Mix categories — don't post 5 space videos in a row
- Reuse top performers as **templates** (same hook style, new topic)

## Metrics to Watch

- **Avg view duration > 80%** = strong hook
- **Swipe-away in first 3s** = bad hook, recut
- **Shares > likes ratio** = viral signal, double down on that format
