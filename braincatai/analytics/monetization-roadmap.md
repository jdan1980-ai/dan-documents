## 💰 Real revenue math

### Production cost per video

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Nano Banana × 8 images | ~$5 |
| Veo 3 × 7 clips + Universal Sc 8 (reusable) | $35-55 |
| ElevenLabs VO | $1-3 |
| Suno music | $2 |
| **Total per video** | **$43-65** |

**Weekly cost (5 videos):** ~$215-325.

### Revenue per view (Shorts ad revenue, monetized)

| Views | Revenue at $0.05 RPM | Revenue at $0.10 RPM |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------|
| 100k | $5 | $10 |
| 500k | $25 | $50 |
| 1M | $50 | $100 |
| 10M (Tier 2 minimum 90d) | $500 | $1,000 |

**Reality check:** Tier 2 90-day minimum (10M views) = $500–1,000 over 3 months = **$170–330/month at the threshold**. Channel barely breaks even on production at this level. Real money starts at 30M+ views/90d.

### Channel memberships (Tier 1+)

Average earning: 5% of subscribers convert at $4.99/month tier. At 1,000 subs that's **~$245/month** before fees. Better margin than Shorts ads.

### Sponsorships (Tier 2+ with track record)

Cat-niche cpm range: $15-40 per 1,000 views per integration. At 1M-view video + brand integration: $15,000-40,000 one-time. **Realistic only after 50-100k subs.**

## 🎯 Optimization levers (sorted by ROI)

### 🔥 Highest-leverage levers

1. **Pre-scripting keyword research (NEW POLICY needed)** — pull vidIQ keyword score BEFORE writing every new script. We wasted time writing `cats-hear-you-blinking` (score 0) when we could have led with `your-cat-sees-you-as-giant-cat` (50k searches, score 71). **Estimated upside: 2-3× audience capture per video.**
2. **One viral attempt/week** — pick ONE topic each week with vidIQ score 70+ AND competitor evidence (someone <5k subs hit >100k views recently). Concentrate effort on that one. **Estimated upside: 1 viral hit unlocks Tier 2.**
3. **Engagement boost in first hour** — pin comment + reply to first 10-15 comments. Algorithm sees active thread → pushes harder. **Estimated upside: +30-50% reach in 24h window.**

### 🟡 Mid-leverage levers

4. **Reusable Scientist-Brain base shot** — Sc 3 in every video is "Brain in lab coat at desk + chart". Generate the base ONCE, swap the chart per video as Google Vids overlay. **Saves ~1 Veo render per video × 5 videos/week = $30-50/week.**
5. **Thumbnail A/B testing** — YouTube allows 3 thumbnail variants per video. Every script already has 1 main + 2 alt concepts → use them. **Estimated upside: 10-30% click-through-rate improvement.**
6. **Reuse end-card** — already done via `end-card.md`. ✅
7. **Reuse Universal Sc 8** — already done via `_universal-scene-8.md`. ✅

### 🟢 Low-leverage but free

8. **Pinned comment update** — every new script's pinned comment goes to `pinned-comments.md`. ✅ already locked.
9. **Daily cadence locked** — algorithm rewards consistency. ✅
10. **Hashtag + description templates locked** — consistent metadata helps indexing. ✅

### 🔵 Longer-term levers

11. **Longform compilation from Shorts (alt monetization path)** — once we have 5-7 published cat Shorts, bundle the best into a 10-min "Top 7 cat psychology facts you didn't know" longform. **Each minute viewed counts toward 4000 watch hours = alt path to Tier 2.** Estimated effort: 1-2h editing per compilation, can do once/month.
12. **RU mirror channel** — every script already has RU titles in `Мир Глазами Кошек` style. Set up `@braincatru` with same video assets + Russian VO → 2× exposure at low marginal cost. **Wait until EN channel hits Tier 1 to avoid splitting focus.**
13. **Sponsorship outreach** — once channel hits 5k subs, approach cat food/litter brands. Cat niche has high CPM. **Wait for Tier 1+.**

## 🚨 Anti-patterns (things that destroy monetization)

- ❌ Off-niche videos → channel-fit demotion (already cost us 40-50× traffic). **NEVER do this again.**
- ❌ Bulk-uploading 8 Veo generations simultaneously → quality drift (Brain loses character consistency). Generate one scene at a time, approve, then proceed.
- ❌ Skipping pinned comment → loses engagement window in first hour.
- ❌ Generic thumbnails (no text plate, no arrow) → low CTR vs competitor formula.
- ❌ Long-form content in the wrong rubric → audience confusion (channel demoted again).
- ❌ Burning Veo credits on text-heavy scenes (V-E-T letters, charts) without using Google Vids overlay fallback → 2-3 retakes per scene wasted.

## 💸 Break-even target

At current production cost ($43-65/video) × 5 videos/week ≈ $250/week ≈ **$1,000/month**.

**Need monthly revenue of $1,000+ to break even on production alone (no time/effort accounting).**

Routes to $1,000/month:
- **Shorts ad revenue:** ~10M views/month (Tier 2 + push)
- **Channel memberships:** ~250 active members at $4.99 = $1,247 (need ~5,000 subs at 5% conversion)
- **Sponsorships:** 1-2 brand integrations/month at $500-2,000 each (need 10k+ subs)

**Realistic target month for break-even:** **6-9 months in** (if recovery goes well + at least one viral hit).
