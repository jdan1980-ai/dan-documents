### 🗣️ Where I DISAGREE with YouTube AI (debate rule applied)

**Disagreement 1 — "Нет проблем с рекомендациями"**

AI says no technical issue. Formally true (no platform bug). But **the data shows a step-function drop**:

| Date | Daily Δ views |
|------|---------------|
| 6 мая | +1,256 |
| **7 мая** | **+97** ⬇️ (13× drop in ONE day) |
| 8-11 мая | 17-44/day |
| 12 мая | +4 (lowest point) |

This is NOT "competition" or "interest fluctuation" — that produces gradual decay. **A 13× one-day drop after the Vagus Nerve release is classic channel-fit demotion** — the algorithm re-categorized the channel based on subscriber audience-mismatch signals (cat-audience skipping non-cat videos).

AI doesn't acknowledge demotion as such because corporate framing avoids the word. But behaviorally, the algorithm did exactly that.

**Disagreement 2 — "5 постоянных зрителей — нормально для нового канала"**

5 returning / 2,856 unique = **0.175% retention rate**.

Benchmark for healthy small cat-channel at this stage: 2-5%. We're **10-30× below benchmark**.

Probable cause: the 37 subs were partly attracted by non-cat content (Sky Blue / Brain Hacks), so they don't return for pure cat content. The TRUE on-brand subscriber base is probably ~25-30, not 37. This is fine — algorithm prefers a smaller identity-aligned base over a confused larger one.

**Disagreement 3 — "Алгоритм вас активно находит" (current tense)**

AI uses 28-day window. Most of those 2,856 unique viewers came in **April + first week of May** during ramp-up. After 7 мая daily views are essentially zero (4-25/day). AI doesn't show the weekly breakdown that would reveal the post-7-мая collapse.

## 💡 New opportunities AI surfaced (worth adopting)

### Opportunity #1 — Returning viewers as primary KPI

**What I notice:** we focused on views/subs/watch hours. AI correctly identifies **returning viewers** as the channel-health metric. We have 5 / 2,856 = 0.175%.

**Why it matters:** returning viewers = the strongest signal to the algorithm that "audience loves this content". Without returning viewers even a viral hit doesn't compound into long-term growth.

**Lift estimate:** doubling returning viewers (5 → 10-15) → +30-50% reach over 30 days.

**Effort:** medium — requires serial content + first-hour comment replies.

**Action:** add `returningViewers` to the monthly check-in schedule in `monetization-roadmap.md`.

### Opportunity #2 — Mini-series for return-viewer compounding

**What I notice:** AI's serialization point is good. Our current scripts are standalone — no explicit "watch next" hook.

**Proposed mini-series from backlog:**
- **"Cat Body Language Decoded"** — 5-7 videos: ears / tail / pupils / whiskers / paws / fur / posture. Each video stands alone but ends with "Tomorrow we decode [next part]."
- **"4 Stare Types"** — `why-cats-stare-at-you` introduces 4 types → deep-dive video on each (Demand / Love / Decode / Predator)
- **"Why Your Cat Does That"** — recurring rubric with connecting hook

**Implementation:** Sc 8 CTA modified to call out the NEXT video in the series instead of generic "Subscribe — Brain's got more".

Example: instead of generic "Subscribe — Brain's got more cat decoding," → "Tomorrow we decode what the tail flick means. Don't miss it."

**Lift:** 2-3× increase in returning viewers over 4-6 weeks.

**Effort:** low (modify Sc 8 VO per series) + medium (write series-coherent backlog).

### Opportunity #3 — Emotion-close-up thumbnails for CTR

**What I notice:** AI suggests emotion close-ups. Our current thumbnail spec = plate + arrow + cat. We could amplify with **strong emotion on the cat's face** (shock / confusion / amusement) crammed close.

Competitor "Мир Глазами Кошек" leans into this — cat emotions are the visual anchor.

**Action:** A/B test the next published video with an emotion-close-up variant (Brain's shocked face from Sc 1 or Sc 4 cropped tight) against the standard plate-and-arrow.

**Lift:** AI estimates 10-30% CTR improvement; could be higher.

**Effort:** low — extract a frame from already-generated Veo clip and add the text plate over the cropped face in Canva.
