## What worked

- **Topic strength** — Doorway Effect is universally relatable (everyone has experienced it), perfect for Shorts
- **Thumbnail / brand recognition** — Brain character + "Brain" tag on collar getting recognized — series identity is forming
- **Hook gets people in** — 35.96% chose this video from the feed, that's well above average

## What killed retention

### 1. Mid-video static shot (00:22–00:34)

Same framing of Brain explaining theory for 12 seconds. No camera move, no cut, no new prop. Shorts viewers can't sit through 10+ seconds without something visually changing. This single dead zone bled 50% of audience.

### 2. The hook isn't the moneyshot

The most striking visual in the video is at **00:18** — Brain "shoots a memory beam" / lightbulb moment. But the cold open was Brain entering the room (setup). Should have started with the beam shot — bigger visual punch in the first 2 seconds.

### 3. End card overloaded (00:42–00:48)

Multiple text overlays — **SUBSCRIBE / THE END / THANK YOU** — stacked on screen at once. Visual noise. Some of them covered Brain's face.

### 4. Filler phrases in the middle

VO at 00:26–00:28 has phrases that don't add a new beat — they could be cut to get to the lightbulb-moment payoff at 00:36 faster.

## Concrete fixes for next batch

These are now baked into [`director-checklist.md`](../director-checklist.md):

- ✅ **Cold open = visual moneyshot.** Always identify the most striking shot in the script and lead with it. Setup is for scene 2+.
- ✅ **No shot held >5 sec without a camera move OR new prop/pose change.** Static-emotion shots are forbidden mid-video.
- ✅ **Slow digital zoom-in on "secret info" reveals.** Push 5–8% closer at the key insight beat to create "you're being let in on something" feeling.
- ✅ **End card = ONE phrase.** "Follow Brain for more brain hacks". NEVER stack SUBSCRIBE + THANK YOU + THE END at once. Don't cover Brain's face with overlay.
- ✅ **Trim filler phrases.** Read VO out loud — every word must earn its place. Cut anything that doesn't add a new fact or beat.
