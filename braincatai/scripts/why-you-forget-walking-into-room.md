## Full Voiceover (whole video, single block — paste into Google Vids voiceover)

```
You walk into a room... and forget why. Don't worry — there's a name for this. It's called the Doorway Effect. And it's not your fault. Every time you walk through a door... your brain hits a button. Like "save and close." Old room? Filed away. New room? Clean slate. Your brain decides anything from the old room is less important. So it forgets it on purpose. That's why you forget what you came in for. Your brain didn't break. It just... moved on. And here's the trick. Walk back where you started. The thought comes right back. So next time? Don't blame yourself. Blame the door. Follow Brain... for more brain hacks.
```
