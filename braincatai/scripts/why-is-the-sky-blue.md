## Voiceover (clean read)

```
The sky isn't actually blue.
Your eyes are lying to you. Here's why.
Sunlight looks white, but it's actually every color mixed together —
red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet.
When sunlight hits Earth's atmosphere, tiny air molecules scatter
the shorter wavelengths — blue and violet — way more than the others.
Your eyes see more blue than violet, so the sky looks blue.
On Mars? The sky is red. Same physics, different air.
Follow for more facts that break your brain.
```

Word count: **89 words** ≈ 45 sec at 120 wpm (read with energy → ~42 sec).
