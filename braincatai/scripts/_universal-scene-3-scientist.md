## 🎬 Animation prompt (Veo 3) — ОДИН РАЗ

```
SHOT: Static medium close-up, eye-level, Brain in lab coat at the lab desk with a clean empty cream-white wall behind him. Brain is a BRIGHT ORANGE TABBY KITTEN with vivid orange fur (#F2994A) and darker tabby stripes — NOT grey, NOT silver. Brain has BRIGHT EMERALD GREEN eyes (#3DDC84) — not brown, not amber.

TIME 0–2s: Brain in his small white lab coat (brown collar with gold heart tag clearly visible at the V-cut neckline) at the lab desk, facing camera with a confident teaching expression. Tail flicks once behind him. Gold heart tag catches the warm light.

TIME 2–4.5s: Brain glances briefly back-and-up toward the empty wall behind him (where the per-video chart will be overlaid in editing) with a knowing nod, eyebrows lifted in teaching mode. One front paw gestures casually upward toward the wall. Then he returns his gaze to camera.

TIME 4.5–7s: Brain holds eye contact with camera, one ear flicks forward, gives a small confident teacher-nod. Gold heart tag glints. Body composed and professional.

FUR COLOR RULE (strict): Brain's fur is BRIGHT WARM ORANGE (#F2994A) with darker burnt-sienna tabby stripes throughout 7 seconds. NOT grey, NOT silver, NOT washed-out.

EYE COLOR RULE (strict): BRIGHT EMERALD GREEN (#3DDC84) throughout. NOT brown, NOT amber.

ANATOMY RULE (strict): Brain has exactly 4 paws — 2 front, 2 back. AT MOST ONE front paw raised at a time. NEVER 5 paws or extra limbs.

MOUTH RULE (strict): Mouth stays completely closed the entire 7 seconds. No lip-sync, no chewing, no chattering.

WARDROBE RULE (strict): Brain wears a small clean white lab coat over his brown leather collar throughout. The brown collar and gold heart-shaped "Brain" tag MUST remain clearly visible at the V-cut neckline — the lab coat does NOT cover them. Gold-framed glasses stay on.

EMPTY-WALL RULE (strict — critical for reuse): The wall directly behind Brain MUST stay completely empty — no chart, no diagram, no chalkboard, no text, no poster, no wall art. Pure clean cream-white surface. This is intentional negative space for a per-video chart overlay to be added in Google Vids editing.

CONSISTENCY RULE (strict): Preserve Brain's young Pixar kitten proportions throughout — round baby-face, plump cheeks, big baby-eyes, small button nose, soft cartoon shapes. NOT photorealistic.

STYLE: Pixar 3D CARTOON render, cinematic warm lab daylight, vertical 9:16, soft depth of field.
```

## 🖼️ Per-video chart overlay (Google Vids step-by-step)

After dropping `assets/universal-scene-3-scientist.mp4` into Sc 3 timeline position, add chart overlay:

1. **Generate the chart artwork separately** (one of three ways):
   - **Option A — Nano Banana**: prompt "Soft pastel cartoon wall chart on cream-white background showing [specific content for this video], Pixar 3D render, vertical 9:16 framing for upper-right placement, transparent or matching cream-white background." Cheaper than Veo 3 — single Nano Banana image.
   - **Option B — Canva**: hand-design a clean pastel chart (4-min job).
   - **Option C — Google Vids built-in text/shape tools**: bare-bones but free.

2. **In Google Vids:** add the chart as an image/text overlay positioned in the upper-right behind Brain's head (where the empty wall is in the universal clip).

3. **Animate the overlay** with a soft pop-in at TIME 2s (when Brain glances back at the wall) and pulse/glow as needed for the video's beat.

4. **If the chart needs revealing in sequence** (e.g. 4 numbered icons glowing one by one in `why-cats-stare-at-you` Sc 3) — add each icon as a separate Google Vids layer with staggered fade-ins.
