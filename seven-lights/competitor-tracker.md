## Risks to watch

- **Monetization:** AI-generated music plus a static or dark screen is the pattern YouTube treats as mass-produced content. Mitigation is in `launch-kit.md` §6 (assemble from many fragments, add own layers, keep project files).
- **Claims:** no "DNA repair", "scientifically proven", "cures", "removes toxins".
