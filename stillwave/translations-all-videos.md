# StillWave Zen — translations for every video

All **104 public videos** (62 long-form + 42 Shorts), pulled via vidIQ on **2026-10-19**, sorted from most to least viewed. Rules and keyword bank: `localization.md`.

## How to use

1. YouTube Studio → Content → open the video → **Subtitles** (Languages) → *Add language*.
2. In the **Title & description** column click *Add*.
3. Paste the title from the list below. For the description, use the template for that language (next section): first line = the translated title, then the template body.
4. Repeat for 8 languages. Start from the top of the list; the top 20 videos hold ~87% of all channel views.

Notes:
- Hashtags from the English Shorts titles are left out of the translations (they look like spam in other languages). The English title stays as is.
- `528 Hz DNA Repair` (20 min): the "DNA repair" claim is left out of the translations. It's a medical claim YouTube can flag, and the "love frequency / open your heart" angle works without it.
- The Shorts title `Kayru The secret of the Japanese Fire Dragon` has a typo in English (**Kayru → Karyū**). The translations use the correct spelling; worth fixing the English one too.
