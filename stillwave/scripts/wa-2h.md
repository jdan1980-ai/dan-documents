## §0 — Positioning & pre-production

- 和 (WA) is a top-tier Kanji-Concept — one of the defining words of Japanese culture (harmony, balance; the kanji even means "Japanese"). Broad wellness pull: harmony / balance / inner peace.
- **Differentiate from NAGOMI (和み, done):** NAGOMI = the *softened, soothed* feeling (a mood). WA = *harmony / balance* as a principle (things in accord). Different wisdom (和顔愛語 vs NAGOMI's 和敬清寂), different visual (mirror-pond symmetry vs NAGOMI's engawa tea). Keep them clearly apart so they don't read as the same video.
- **Visual idea (locked direction pending user pick):** a perfectly still **mirror pond** — the scene and its reflection meet in symmetry at the waterline = a literal image of 和 (balance). Alt: a raked **zen rock garden** (karesansui, harmony of stone/gravel/space).
- **🇺🇦** 和 (WA) — «гармония/равновесие», один из ключевых японских концептов (и сам иероглиф «японского»). Отделяем от NAGOMI (和み — «умиротворение», настроение): WA — принцип согласия/баланса. Другая мудрость (和顔愛語), другой визуал (зеркальный пруд-симметрия). Настроение музыки — тёплое, текучее, «в согласии».

WA (和) in Japanese Culture: A Concise Overview

和 (wa) means "harmony" — and it is so central to Japanese identity that the character itself came to stand for Japan. Prince Shōtoku opened Japan's first constitution in 604 AD with 和を以て貴しと為す, "harmony is to be treasured above all." Wa is not the absence of difference but the art of holding differences in balance — sky and water, stone and moss, self and others, each resting in accord with the rest.
