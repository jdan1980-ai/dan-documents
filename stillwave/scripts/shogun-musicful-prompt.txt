Extremely quiet, hushed, calm Japanese samurai meditation music, pianissimo throughout, like music heard late at night in the empty golden audience hall of an old castle, where the shogun sits alone by candlelight beneath an ancient painted tiger. A low, breathy shakuhachi bamboo flute plays very soft, long, sparse phrases with long silences between them; warm koto is plucked gently, one quiet note at a time, never a repeating pattern, each note left to fade; a deep, dark, warm drone rests underneath like slow breathing. Traditional Japanese pentatonic feeling. Mood: quiet authority, dignity, the weight of command set down for the night, deep calm. Very slow, free tempo, no drums, no percussion, no beats, no vocals, no lyrics, no sudden changes, no loud notes, no bright high sounds, no swelling, no climax. A long composition of about 4 to 5 minutes that stays calm and unchanging, do not end early. The same soft level from start to end, smooth fade in and fade out. Instrumental, high quality, wide stereo.
