Extremely quiet, hushed, calm Japanese samurai meditation music, pianissimo throughout. Like an old storyteller playing alone at night. A Japanese biwa lute plucked very softly and sparsely, single low notes left to ring and fade, never a pattern; a dark warm drone underneath; long silences. Mood: memory, solitude, ancient warriors at rest. Traditional Japanese pentatonic feeling in D minor. Very slow, free tempo, no drums, no percussion, no beats, no vocals, no lyrics, no sudden changes, no bright high sounds, no swelling, no climax. A long composition of about 4 to 5 minutes that stays calm and unchanging, do not end early. The same soft level from start to end, smooth fade in and out. Instrumental, high quality.
