Extremely quiet, hushed, calm Japanese samurai meditation music, pianissimo throughout, like music heard at night in the hall of a great one-eyed warlord, his black armor resting by candlelight. Traditional Japanese pentatonic feeling. Mood: a warrior's quiet strength, memory, deep calm. Very slow, free tempo, no drums, no percussion, no beats, no vocals, no lyrics, no sudden changes, no bright high sounds, no swelling, no climax. A long composition of about 4 to 5 minutes that stays calm and unchanging, do not end early. The same soft level from start to end, smooth fade in and out. Instrumental, high quality.
