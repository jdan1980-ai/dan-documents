## §0 — Positioning & pre-production

- **Why this concept:** 一期一会 is one of the most recognizable Japanese phrases in the West (self-help, mindfulness, tea culture) — same "Zen concept + crossover appeal" formula that made MUSHIN break out. Broad wellness pull: present moment / mindfulness / gratitude / impermanence.
- **Differentiate the visual:** our recent heroes are outdoor water scenes (WA mirror pond, SHOSHIN round window, KOMOREBI forest). ICHIGO ICHIE goes **indoors → the tea ceremony**, the literal origin of the phrase. A lone tea master before a single steaming bowl. The **rising steam** is the whole idea made visible — it forms once, drifts, and is never the same again. Distinct silhouette, distinct palette (warm wabi-sabi interior), same back-to-camera signature figure.
- **Wisdom phrase is NOT 一期一会** (that phrase is already used as KOMOREBI's overlay, and the overlay must differ from the title concept). Chosen: **日々是好日** (every day is a good day) — a Zen/tea-ceremony line about meeting each day fully. See §6a.
- **🇺🇦** 一期一会 — «одна встреча, один раз в жизни»: каждый миг неповторим, проживи его сейчас. Из чайной церемонии. Сильный кроссовер (как MUSHIN). Визуал уводим В ПОМЕЩЕНИЕ — чайная церемония, исток фразы: мастер спиной + одна дымящаяся чаша, **пар = неповторимость мига**. Отличается от наших прудов. Wisdom-фраза — не сам концепт, а **日々是好日** (§6a).

## §1 — Suno prompts (10 copy-paste variants — deeply meditative, FLAT dynamics, ametric)

> 🔒 ALL variants: **no percussion, no BPM, free time** + the locked length cue AND the locked **flat-dynamics cue** (both in the tail). **🛌 The music must stay MEDITATIVE and NEVER build** — listeners fall asleep to it; any rise/swell wakes them and they drop off. One soft even unchanging level from first second to last: no crescendo, no swell, no climax, no "section B deepens/widens." Instruments drift in/out and change colour, volume never grows. Tempo in words only. Plucked/струнные — `never a repeating pattern`, long gaps. Each variant a DIFFERENT opening (anti-Content-ID). Warm, intimate, a touch of *mono no aware* (the gentle ache of transience) — koto, shakuhachi, singing bowl, temple bell, warm strings, harp, felt piano, all blending.
> **🇺🇦** Все 10 — глубоко медитативные, **ровная динамика без нарастаний** (иначе слушатель просыпается). Без ударных/BPM, свободный темп, разные вступления, хвост с подсказкой на длину + ровную динамику. Тон тёплый, интимный, с лёгкой печалью быстротечности.
