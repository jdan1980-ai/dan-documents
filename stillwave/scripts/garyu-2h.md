## §0 — Positioning & pre-production

- **Why this concept:** 臥龍 is the one samurai idea that is *about stillness* rather than about combat — which means it belongs on a meditation-music channel instead of fighting the epic/battle cluster for attention. It gives the series its thesis in two characters, and it draws in one frame.
- **Why it opens the series:** the pilot has to establish the locked hero (warrior at rest · bare head · fearsome helmet facing the viewer · swords racked · painted dragon asleep · smoke dragon awake). GARYŪ *is* that frame — the other four episodes are variations on it.
- **Differentiate the visual:** our recent heroes are outdoor and pale — WA's mirror pond, SHOSHIN's round window, KOMOREBI's forest, ICHIGO ICHIE's dawn tea room. GARYŪ goes **dark, interior, night, high-contrast**: black lacquer and gold leaf, one lantern, one rust-red accent. This is the strongest thumbnail contrast the channel has ever had, and the menpō mask catching a rim light is the thing that stops the scroll.
- **The eye-catcher is the mask, the motion is the smoke.** Those are two different jobs. The helmet sells the click; the coiling smoke dragon holds the watch-time.
- **Wisdom phrase is NOT 臥龍** (the overlay must differ from the title concept). Chosen: **静中動** — movement within stillness. See §6a.
- **🇺🇦** 臥龍 — «лежащий дракон»: огромная сила, свёрнутая и намеренно не пущенная в ход. В классике так называли скрытого мастера, который ещё не вышел на сцену (прозвище Чжугэ Ляна). У нас это воин ПОСЛЕ боя: шлем снят, мечи отложены, а вся опасность осталась в комнате. Это единственная самурайская идея, которая **про покой**, а не про схватку, — поэтому она законно живёт на медитативном канале и не дерётся за внимание с epic-battle кластером. Визуал уводим в **тёмный ночной интерьер** (чёрный лак, сусальное золото, один фонарь, ржаво-красный акцент) — максимальный контраст превью за всю историю канала. **Маска продаёт клик, дым держит досмотр.**

## §1 — Suno prompts (8 copy-paste variants — meditative, FLAT dynamics)

> 🔒 **The music must NEVER build.** One soft even unchanging level from the first second to the last: no crescendo, no swell, no climax, no "section B deepens." Instruments drift in and out and change colour; volume never grows. Each variant has a DIFFERENT opening (anti-Content-ID). Tempo in words only.
>
> 🔒 **Two tails, and they are not interchangeable.** Variants 1–5 use **Tail A** (percussion-free, sleep-safe). Variants 6–8 use **Tail B**, which permits *one* low taiko heartbeat at a constant, unchanging level — the drum is the samurai genre signal, and dropping it entirely costs the series its identity. Tail B still forbids every kind of build. **Rotation: run 1–5 across roughly two-thirds of the album and 6–8 across the rest, never two same-variant tracks adjacent.**
