## §0 — Positioning & pre-production

- **Style — locked (per the channel's Kanji-Concept competitor-template spec):** photoreal cinematic, like a still frame from a Kurosawa film — NOT Ghibli painterly, NOT stylized. Lone figure back-to-camera, face never visible. ONE massive symbolic visual hook dominating the upper two-thirds. Single dramatic location. One dominant warm palette on a muted dark background. Mood: profound stillness and mystery, not cheerful relaxation.
- **Human anchor:** a lone monk in seiza meditation at the water's edge — not a samurai (no swords needed; the theme is dissolving into stillness, not martial readiness). Back to camera, face never visible, per series convention.
- **Visual hook — the dissolving enso:** a giant traditional ink-brush ENSO (zen circle) painted on a hanging silk scroll suspended above a still mountain lake at first light. Unlike a normal crisp ENSO, THIS one is caught at the exact moment its lower arc dissolves — the brushstroke's ink feathering and bleeding outward into fine mist and droplets that fall and merge seamlessly into the lake's own surface mist below, so the circle's boundary visibly disappears into the world around it. This is the one deliberate visual difference from MUSHIN's own (crisper, intact) enso — MUGA's circle is caught mid-dissolution, literalizing "no-self" rather than "no-mind." The monk's own silhouette, seated at the water's edge in the lower third, has its outer edge similarly softened by the same rising mist, so man and circle both read as dissolving into the same stillness — without overdoing it; he must still read clearly as a person, not literally vanishing.
- **Location:** a still mountain lake at first light (pre-dawn), mirror-flat water, mist rising off the surface.
- **Palette:** muted cool grey-blue pre-dawn tones across most of the frame, with ONE warm amber light source — a sliver of dawn glow on the horizon reflected in the water — as the single warm accent per the spec.
- **🔒 Standard channel rules apply:** no in-image AI text, no airborne particles (mist rising/drifting is fine — it's not a discrete object like a leaf/bird; the ink-mist dissolving off the scroll should read as continuous vapor, never as distinct floating ink-blobs), no watermark language needed in prompts (confirmed to do nothing either way, per the 2026-09-21 rule).
- **Wisdom phrase is NOT 無我 itself** (overlay ≠ title concept, series rule). Chosen: **物我一如** (butsuga ichinyo — "self and the world, one and the same"), a classical Zen/aesthetic idiom describing exactly the muga state — the dissolution of the boundary between subject and object — without naming the concept directly. See §6a.
- **🇺🇦** Прямое тематическое продолжение MUSHIN (無心, топ-1 видео канала, 41.8К просмотров) — то же «состояние сознания», на один концепт глубже: MUSHIN — ум без мыслей, MUGA — растворение самого «я» в действии (истинный поток). Музыка — Suno, не Lyria: MUGA специально копирует именно ту формулу, которая уже сработала (MUSHIN/GAMAN/KIRI/NAGOMI все на Suno), а не переносит непроверенный пайплайн KAMI (сейчас самая слабая когорта канала). Визуальный крюк — гигантский энсо на свитке над горным озером на рассвете, но в момент растворения нижней дуги в тумане (в отличие от целого энсо у MUSHIN) — буквализирует именно «не-я», а не «не-ум». Мудрость — не сам 無我, а 物我一如 («я и мир — одно и то же»).

## §12 — Community Post (day-of; reuse thumbnail)

```
MUGA (無我) in Japanese Culture: A Concise Overview

無我 (muga), literally "no-self," names the Zen state one level deeper than mushin (無心, "no-mind"): where mushin empties the mind of thought, muga dissolves the self itself into the act — the doer and the doing become indistinguishable. It is the philosophical root of what modern psychology calls "flow state," long described in Japanese arts (calligraphy, archery, tea ceremony) centuries before the term existed. The classical idiom 物我一如 ("self and world, one and the same") captures the same idea — the boundary between subject and object simply disappearing. Here a lone monk sits at the edge of a still mountain lake as a giant ink circle above him dissolves into the same mist he is beginning to disappear into.
```
