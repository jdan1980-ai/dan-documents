FUDOSHIN (不動心) means "the immovable mind" — a state of unshakable inner steadiness prized in the martial arts and in Zen. Its name echoes Fudō Myōō, the "Immovable Wisdom King" of Buddhism, who sits serene amid flames, and the ascetic practice of takigyō, meditating beneath a crashing waterfall. Fudōshin is not coldness or rigidity; it is a rooted composure — a mind that stays calm, clear, and centered no matter what storm strikes it.
