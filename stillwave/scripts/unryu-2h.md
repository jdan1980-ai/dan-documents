## §1 — Suno prompts (10 copy-paste variants — deeply meditative, FLAT dynamics, ametric)

> 🔒 ALL variants: **no percussion, no BPM, free time** + the locked length cue AND the locked **flat-dynamics cue** (both in the tail). **🛌 The music must stay MEDITATIVE and NEVER build** — listeners fall asleep to it; any rise/swell wakes them and they drop off. One soft even unchanging level from first second to last. Instruments drift in/out and change colour, volume never grows. Tempo in words only. Plucked/струнные — `never a repeating pattern`, long gaps. Each variant a DIFFERENT opening (anti-Content-ID). Cool, high, spacious, clear — koto, shakuhachi, singing bowl, temple bell, warm strings, harp, felt piano, all blending, evoking thin mountain air and open sky.
> **🇺🇦** Все 10 — глубоко медитативные, **ровная динамика без нарастаний**. Без ударных/BPM, свободный темп, разные вступления, хвост с подсказкой на длину + ровную динамику. Тон холодный, высокий, простор — «горный воздух и открытое небо».

雲龍 (unryū) — "cloud dragon" — is one of Japan's most painted dragon images, found on temple ceilings and gates across the country (the great ceiling dragons of Kenninji and Tenryūji among the most famous). The dragon does not fly by its own effort — legend holds it rises by riding the clouds it gathers around itself, an image later adopted as a metaphor for a hero who rises the moment circumstance turns in their favor.
