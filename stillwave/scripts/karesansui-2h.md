## §0 — Positioning & pre-production

**Why this video (VidIQ, 2026-10-29):**
- `zen garden music` — ~31.7K searches/month, **+44% growth**, competition 34 (low). One of the few growing phrases in the niche, while `japanese zen music` (-32%) and `zen meditation music` (-68%) are falling.
- `zen garden` — ~46.8K/month, +16%. Nobody searches "karesansui", so the title carries **Zen Garden** in plain English.
- Fits the locked rule (CLAUDE.md, 2026-10-08): a **recognizable icon** (everyone knows a Zen rock garden), not an obscure named figure.
- Breakout check: a "Calming Japanese Garden Ambience" video hit 190K views on a 4.2K-sub channel; the biggest niche hits (TAO 1.7M, KUMAMICHI 2.2M, KOI 849K) all carry ONE strong central image.

**Visual hook (the fix for "sand and stones read flat"):** seen from a raised temple veranda at dawn, the raked gravel lines sweep into **one giant circle — a sand ENSO — around a single massive moss-topped stone**, like a ripple around an island. Behind the low clay garden wall, a **huge pale dawn sun** sits low in thick morning mist. The monk sits on the wooden veranda in the lower third, back to camera.

**Concept guardrails:**
- Do NOT build the copy around 間 (Ma) — MA is already published. Meaning here = **stone is eternity, sand is the endless ocean**.
- Japanese temple bells only (rin, bonshō) — **not Tibetan singing bowls**.
- No water, rain or wind described in the Lyria prompts (2026-09-16 rule). The optional rain bed is a separate CC0 layer (§2).

**🇺🇦 RU:** Почему это видео: «zen garden music» ищут ~31,7 тыс. раз в месяц с ростом +44% и низкой конкуренцией, а общие запросы ниши падают. Слово «karesansui» никто не ищет, поэтому в названии английское «Zen Garden». Сад камней — узнаваемый образ, как требует правило канала. Визуальный крюк: граблёный гравий складывается в гигантский песочный круг-энсо вокруг одного огромного мшистого камня, за стеной сада — огромное бледное рассветное солнце в тумане, монах на веранде в нижней трети. Концепцию не строим на Ма (видео MA уже было): камень — вечность, песок — бесконечный океан. Колокола только японские, без тибетских чаш. Вода, дождь и ветер в промты музыки не идут.

KARESANSUI (枯山水) in Japanese Culture: A Concise Overview

Karesansui (枯山水), literally "dry mountain water," is the Japanese dry landscape garden, developed in Zen temples from the 14th and 15th centuries. There is no water in it at all: raked white gravel stands for the sea and flowing rivers, and carefully placed stones stand for mountains and islands. The most famous, at Ryōan-ji in Kyoto, holds fifteen stones arranged so that from any point on the veranda at least one stays hidden. Monks rake the gravel every morning, and the garden is not meant to be walked through, only looked at — a place to sit still until the mind becomes as quiet as the stone.
