WABI SABI (侘寂) in Japanese Culture: A Concise Overview

WABI SABI (侘寂) joins wabi — the beauty of humble simplicity — and sabi — the beauty that time and wear bring. Where Western aesthetics chase symmetry and polish, wabi-sabi finds its deepest beauty in the cracked, the faded, and the incomplete. Its most famous expression is kintsugi (金継ぎ), the art of repairing broken pottery with gold: the break is never hidden but honored as part of the object's history. As the tea master Sen no Rikyū taught, perfection leaves no room for the heart — a garden is swept, and then a few leaves are shaken back onto the moss.
