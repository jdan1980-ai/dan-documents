Nara was Japan's first long-lasting capital, from 710 to 784, when Buddhism and the imperial court shaped the country's early identity. Tōdai-ji's Great Buddha Hall, one of the largest wooden buildings in the world, still shelters its giant bronze Buddha. In the mist and moonlight of a Nara night, the old stillness feels close, as if the forest has been waiting for you to slow down.
