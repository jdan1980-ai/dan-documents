## §0 — Positioning & pre-production

- **🔒 KAMI series invariants (carried over):** each god manifests as a colossal figure formed of LIVING LIGHT/MIST — never a statue, never a carved idol, never a painted mural. A lone human figure anchors scale. Each god gets its own **signature light colour** — **AMATERASU = warm golden-amber sunrise light** (distinct from Tsukuyomi's pale silver-blue and Hachiman's colour).
- **🔒 Pose matches domain (locked 2026-09-01, carried from TSUKUYOMI):** Hachiman stood, armored, active guardian. Tsukuyomi sat withdrawn in zazen. **Amaterasu is the opposite temperament of Tsukuyomi — she is warmth, radiance, and life bursting back into the world after darkness (the Ama-no-Iwato myth).** She is therefore STANDING, arms open and rising, emerging as if stepping back out of the cave into the light she herself is made of — an active, ascendant pose, not a still one. The human anchor, by contrast, stays seated in quiet meditation (the video is literally titled "Morning Meditation") — so the CONTRAST is between the still monk and the rising, radiant goddess above him, rather than between two seated figures.
- **AMATERASU — visual hook:** a lone monk sits in quiet dawn meditation at the edge of a mountain temple terrace, facing a mist-filled valley of rice terraces just catching first light. Rising out of the sunrise itself beyond the valley, a colossal standing figure of Amaterasu takes shape — robed in flowing warm gold-amber light, arms open and rising as if just stepping out from behind a great stone doorway (the Ama-no-Iwato), haloed in radiant golden sunburst rays, one hand holding the sacred mirror Yata no Kagami (its surface catching and doubling the sunrise), the other holding a sheaf of golden rice stalks. Two sacred roosters — her companions, whose crowing is said to have helped call her out of the cave — stand calmly at her feet, per the myth. The god's visible form, the mirror, and the roosters are "made of" the sunrise light and drifting golden mist; the lower folds of her robe dissolve into the mist over the valley.
- **🔒 No falling/floating petals, no random airborne particles — do not mention them even to forbid them** (locked rule). Tsukuyomi's signature companions were the two still moon rabbits; Amaterasu's are two still, calm sacred roosters (per the myth of the cockerels' crow luring her from the cave) — a deliberately different companion-pair so the series doesn't repeat itself.
- **🔒 Any light source that should stay constant (the rising sun, the manifestation's glow) must already be present from the first frame** — never fading in mid-loop (SUIRYU lesson).
- **No in-image text anywhere** — handled only in post via the compose script.
- **Wisdom phrase is NOT 天照 itself** (overlay ≠ title concept). Chosen: **一陽来復** (ichiyō raifuku — literally "the one yang returns"), a classical idiom for the turning point when light and warmth return after the darkest stretch — directly mirrors the Ama-no-Iwato myth of light returning to the world. See §6a.
- **🔒 Detail-object variety (carried from TSUKUYOMI rule):** the breather/detail shot must NOT default to the stone lantern. Here the natural choice is the **Yata no Kagami mirror** (already the god's own attribute) plus a **second, different** detail — golden rice stalks — so neither detail slot repeats an earlier video's prop.
- **🇺🇦** Третье видео серии KAMI — AMATERASU, богиня Солнца. Миф: после ссоры с Сусаноо она укрылась в пещере Ама-но-Ивато, погрузив мир во тьму; боги выманили её танцем Амэ-но-Удзумэ и священным зеркалом Ята-но-Кагами, которое отразило её же сияние — свет вернулся. Поза бога — противоположна Цукуёми: не сидящая и отстранённая, а **вставшая, с раскрытыми поднимающимися руками**, как будто она только что вышла из пещеры на свет (контраст со спокойно сидящим монахом внизу — тот всё ещё в медитации, отсюда и подзаголовок "Morning Meditation"). Компаньоны — два священных петуха (не зайцы, не голуби), деталь-объект — зеркало Ята-но-Кагами + золотые колосья риса (не фонарь, не лотос). Мудрость оверлея — 一陽来復 («один ян возвращается» — свет и тепло возвращаются после тьмы), не сам 天照.

AMATERASU (天照) in Japanese Culture: A Concise Overview

天照 (Amaterasu Ōmikami), the goddess of the sun, was born from the left eye of the primordial god Izanagi, sister to Tsukuyomi (the moon) and Susanoo (storms and sea). Ruler of Takamagahara, the heavenly plain, and ancestor of Japan's Imperial line, she is Shinto's most revered deity. After a bitter conflict with her brother Susanoo, she withdrew into the cave Ama-no-Iwato, plunging the entire world into darkness — the other gods lured her back out with a wild dance by Ame-no-Uzume and the sacred mirror Yata no Kagami, hung on a sakaki tree, which reflected her own radiance back at her so convincingly that she stepped out to look, and light returned to the world. She remains closely tied to warmth, rice cultivation, and weaving. Here a lone monk sits facing a misty valley as that same sun goddess takes shape above it at dawn.
