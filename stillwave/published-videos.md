## Key insights — refreshed 2026-09-26

### 🏆 Top performers (all-time)

1. **MUSHIN — 無心 — 41,792 views** ⭐⭐⭐ Still #1 by an enormous margin (~17x the #2 video), though the viral spike has flattened since early July — this is now a steady long-tail earner, not an active climb.
2. **GAMAN — 我慢 — 2,456 views.** The channel's clear #2, and the only other video in four digits.
3. **KIRI — 霧 — 1,037 views.** Old-format ("иероглиф") era benchmark, still holding up months later.
4. **NAGOMI — 和み — 827 views.** 🆕 Quietly the best-performing video of the *current* Kanji-Concept template era — outperforming both WABI-SABI and IKIGAI. Worth studying why (title/thumb/timing) and considering a spiritual sequel.
5. **852 Hz Monks' Secret — 731 views.** Still the strongest Hz/Healing Hour video by far.
6. **WABI-SABI — 560 views**, the video that prompted this refresh (45:47 avg view duration / 38% retention on a 2H video — genuinely excellent retention for the format).

### 🎯 Patterns that work

- ✅ **"Letting go / impermanence" theme resonates** — WABI-SABI (560) and MONO NO AWARE (154) both explore accepting impermanence; NAGOMI (827, harmony/comfort) and IKIGAI (638, purpose) round out the top tier of "meaning-of-life" concepts. FUJIN (Letting Go, Release & Inner Peace) is thematically adjacent — worth watching its trajectory.
- ✅ **The original Kanji-Concept template (monk + single symbolic visual hook) still outperforms** — KIRI (1,037), SATORI (692), NAGOMI (827), IKIGAI (638), WABI-SABI (560) all use it and all land well above the channel's ~250-view median.
- ✅ **RYŪ sub-series (dragons) landed solidly mid-pack** — avg **~184 views** across the 5 (129-221 each), a tight, consistent band. No breakout, but no dud either — a reliable, repeatable format.
- ✅ **Shorts can meaningfully outperform their own long-form parent** — GARYŪ's short (275) beat its long-form (129); SEIRYŪ's short (273) is close to its long-form (221). Shorts are pulling real independent weight, not just cross-promo.

### 🚧 Patterns that struggle

- ❌ **KAMI (Gods of Japan) sub-series is the weakest cohort in current production — avg ~126 views** across TSUKUYOMI/AMATERASU/HACHIMAN/BENZAITEN/KANNON/FUJIN (RAIJIN and INARI too new to count yet). That's *below* the RYŪ dragons (~184) and well below the Kanji-Concept template average (~306). Worth investigating: the KAMI visual language (colossal light-spirit deity) is a bigger stylistic swing from the winning "monk + symbolic mural/ENSO" template than RYŪ was, the videos are shorter (1H vs 2H, less total watch-time-per-view opportunity), and Lyria (vs Suno) is a newer, less-proven music pipeline for this channel. None of these are proven causes yet — flagging for a deeper look, not a verdict.
- ❌ **8H videos still dead weight** — 53 and 28 views. Confirmed discontinued, stay discontinued.
- ❌ **639 Hz 3H (90) and other 3H+ non-Kanji-Concept videos** sit well below the channel median — 3H+ format still only works when it's also a Kanji-Concept video (SATORI 3H still got 692).
- ❌ **Tokyo Rain / Power Hour / Pomodoro cluster is soft** — avg ~97 views across 6 videos (Deep Work Marathon 293 carries the average; the rest sit 35-78). This pillar hasn't grown since the original April/May pushes.
