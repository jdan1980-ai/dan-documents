## Use during `GAP: SW` and `CAL:` runs

Before suggesting new themes:

1. Open each channel → check last 5–10 uploads
2. Note title patterns (иероглиф vs Hz vs themed phrase)
3. Note their best-performing recent video
4. Check our `published-videos.md` — what overlaps, what's missing
5. Build the gap list: themes high on competitors, missing from us
