## 3. The three reasons

1. **"Samurai" is a category, not a concept.** The lead slot has to hold a word you can own and repeat — TAO 道, BUDŌ 武道, BYAKKO 白虎, RYŪ 龍. You cannot build a second episode out of "Samurai"; there is nothing to name it.
2. **Bare `samurai` (competition 52.1) drops you in the wrong cluster.** Its SERP is Cyberpunk 2077 SAMURAI Full Album (2.4M), Epic Samurai Battle Music, lofi-hiphop mixes and anime — a different audience with different watch behaviour. Same failure mode as Pomodoro 50/10 landing in Lifestyle instead of Music. `samurai music` (competition 16.1) is a *different, clean* keyword and it belongs in the tail and the tags.
3. **The film-soundtrack lane is closed to us.** The 5.98M-view leader states in its own description that the audio belongs to the Last Samurai soundtrack. Those channels operate on Content ID risk we deliberately don't take (`makoto-the-last-samurai.md` §12).

## 4. The finding that actually matters

**Zen Samurai Meditations** (`UC9JmhLDB2Zu9hLKlrf-15xQ`) runs our exact MAKOTO formula — kanji concept, 2H, Japanese ambient — at 120K–588K per video. MAKOTO got **69**.

The theme is not the problem. Two things are:

- **Series vs one-off.** They have five same-template videos feeding each other through suggested. MAKOTO sits alone between Hz-frequency videos and Pomodoro timers, with no cluster to belong to.
- **Drawable vs abstract concepts.** Dragon, white tiger, carp, turtle each resolve to one thumbnail frame. 誠 "sincerity" and 我慢 "endurance" resolve to nothing, so the thumbnails carry no subject and CTR follows.
