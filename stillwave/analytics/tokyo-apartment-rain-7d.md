## What worked

1. **Title formula** — `[Scene] + [Action Marathon] | [Length] Uninterrupted Vol. N` validated at VidIQ 93/100. Pattern reusable.
2. **48h browse-feed push** — algorithm liked the thumbnail + title combination enough to test on browse for ~2 days.
3. **Series brand isolated to thumbnail + playlist** — keeps title clean for keyword density, lets brand carry through visual layer.

## What didn't

1. **No search ranking established** — view tail (3–10 v/d after Day 3) means no specific search term is sending steady traffic. Need to check Search vs Browse breakdown in YT Studio (not in API).
2. **Like-rate 1.24%** — below the like-rate benchmark from KIRI (3.3%) and 852 Hz (2.1%). Hypothesis: focus-music viewers minimize tab and don't engage with UI, vs meditation viewers who interact more.
3. **Audience comments = 1, off-topic** — pinned comment ("Drop one word — coding, writing, studying, trading") didn't seed a real conversation. Comment CTA might be too constrained or audience not seeing the pin (since most listen tab-out).
4. **VPH peaked at T+11h then collapsed by Day 3.** Suggests Day-1 momentum was thumbnail-driven (browse impressions) but title/CTR doesn't sustain into search.

## Open data gaps (need YouTube Studio, not API)

## Recommendations going into Vol. 2 (Tokyo Snowfall, publishing today May 17)

1. **Keep the title formula** — it works at VidIQ scoring and got 113 views in 48h. No need to redesign.
2. **Pinned-comment v2** — drop the "single word" constraint; try open-ended ("What's in front of you right now?") or seed with controversy ("Coffee or tea while you work?") to invite easy engagement.
3. **Thumbnail strategy stays** — POWER HOUR overlay + locked formula. Don't pivot mid-experiment.
4. **A/B opportunity:** change ONE variable on Vol. 2 — try a `1 Hour Focus Sprint` instead of `1 Hour Uninterrupted` in the title to see if "Sprint" outperforms "Uninterrupted" for CTR. Cheap experiment.
5. **Add a 9:16 Short** companion in 7 days — link discovery from Shorts feed → long. Tokyo Rain doesn't have one yet, neither does Healing Hour.

## 30-day projection

At current 5–10 v/d tail, Tokyo Rain Vol. 1 projects to **~250–290 views at 30d** (June 9). If a Short pulls cross-traffic mid-month, +50–100 plausible. If a Reddit/Discord placement happens, could hit 500. Without intervention, likely 270.

## Decision: POWER HOUR formula is validated for continued production

Despite tail flatness, 161 views in 7 days on a ~50-sub channel **with a discoverable thumbnail + searchable title** is the strongest performance the channel has produced. The format is the new baseline.

Next slot (Vol. 2 today, Vol. 3 within the week) tests:
