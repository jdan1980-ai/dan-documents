### 🏷️ Winning tag patterns (the gold)

**MERSO uses IDENTICAL 10-tag set on EVERY video** (genius — channel-level keyword density):

```
deep work music, deep focus music, work music, focus music, study music,
concentration music, productivity music, background music for work,
music for studying, instrumental music
```

**Power Hour Focus varies tags per video.** Examples by top:

- **TOP-1 (470K views):** `calming nature sounds, soothing relaxation, good morning music, peder b. helland, relajarse, piano music, relaxing piano music, soothing, sleep music, meditation music, calm, soothing music` — **broad cross-niche play** (sleep + meditation + relaxation tags on a focus video!)
- **TOP-2 (181K):** `focus music, ambient music, background work music, ambient, deep focus music, ambient downtempo music, ambient outpost, ambient space music, ambient study music` — **ambient-flavor cluster** (sheet variations of "ambient")
- **TOP-3 (63K):** `deep work music, #chillstep, work music, music for work, sleep music, study music, relaxing music, focus music for work, concentration music, deep focus music, mindfulness, ambient study music` — **mixed with hashtag-as-tag** (`#chillstep`)

**Tactical takeaways for StillWave:**

1. ✅ **Lock 10 core tags channel-wide** like MERSO does (build niche authority)
2. ✅ **Add cross-niche tags** like PHF top-1 — "sleep music" / "meditation music" on focus videos = algo cross-pollination
3. ✅ **Add language variations** ("relajarse" — Spanish; could also add "música para concentrarse", "música para estudiar") — multi-language search reach
4. ✅ **Consider hashtag-as-tag** trick (`#chillstep`, `#lofi`) for genre crossover
5. ✅ **Sheet variations of "ambient"** — "ambient music", "ambient study music", "ambient downtempo music", "ambient space music" — captures every search variant
